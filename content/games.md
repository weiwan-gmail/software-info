## 4. How to dig for online small games (ones you do not already know)

- itch.io by tag: `browser`, `one-button`, `incremental`
- Flashpoint filtered by original site: 4399, Coolmath, Kongregate
- Personal sites like neal.fun (one author, a string of small experiments) are cleaner than portals
- Prototypes demoed in FOSDEM / GDC talks
- Do not treat “another io reskin” as a discovery

When collecting, write: playable as a single-player webpage / needs an account / needs a downloaded launcher.
